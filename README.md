# Bachat Alerts: clickable prototype

A clickable concept prototype for a Paytm product campus case study (Case 3: credit card spends analyser).

Bachat Alerts warns Paytm users before a credit card loses them money: a renewal fee that will not be waived, lounge access that depends on last quarter's spend, or a payment made with the wrong card. It speaks at three moments: paying the card bill, during the billing cycle, and paying at a Paytm QR.

**This is a concept, not a Paytm product.** Names, cards and amounts are illustrative, and nothing is paid.

The prototype is a single static page (`index.html`) with no build step.
